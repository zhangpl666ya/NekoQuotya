Why it's nice to have

      
Cute outside, careful inside

    

    

      
🔒
100% local
Usage is read from your own log files by tokscale. No account, no server, no telemetry.

      
📒
Never loses a session
A per-session ledger keeps your history even after the AI tool cleans up its logs.

      
⚡
Live in seconds
She watches the log folders and reacts within seconds of your next prompt.

      
🎚️
Quota or budget
Subscriptions follow the 5-hour and weekly quota. API billing follows a daily token budget.

      
🧩
Add your own girl
Each character is a folder: pack.json plus a few PNGs. Any tool tokscale supports can get one.

      
🌏
English & 中文
The whole app follows your system language.
